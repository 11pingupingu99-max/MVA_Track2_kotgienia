# MVA Track 2 - 3-minute narration cues v1.0.2

## Slide 1 | 0:00-0:18
Our Track 2 starts from a scorer-confirmed genetic input, not from a drug list. The BUB1B L737-star plus N1002K pair received 100 out of 100 rank points and F-max 1.000 at EPCR 0.78. That validates the ranking, but not phase, mechanism, or therapy.

## Slide 2 | 0:18-0:40
The first gate is phase. The two variants are 10,911 base pairs apart, so short-read read-backed phasing cannot directly connect them. Parental genotyping is the simplest decisive route. Only after phase do we compare WT and N1002K across matched BUBR1 abundance, using chromosome missegregation per completed division as the primary endpoint.

## Slide 3 | 0:40-1:02
That experiment can return abundance-dominant, function-dominant, mixed, or indeterminate. If raising N1002K abundance restores function, a conditional proteostasis test becomes admissible. If a functional defect persists at matched abundance, no approved drug in our audited target space qualifies as a credible causal rescue. We stop repurposing rather than weakening the claim.

## Slide 4 | 1:02-1:27
We also repaired the approved-drug audit. Exact component-aware mapping, approval filtering, and explicit NOT-RUN states replaced the earlier ranking logic. The important result is not a winner: database-rich direct inhibitors and downstream kinases are easy to recover, while desired indirect rescue mechanisms are sparse. Coverage tells us what can be tested; it does not establish therapeutic credibility.

## Slide 5 | 1:27-1:57
NB09 then asks whether a test is pharmacologically credible. Arimoclomol is FDA-approved for NPC, while CHMP issued a negative European opinion in July 2026 and re-examination is ongoing. Recommended-regimen pediatric Cmax is about 1.67 micromolar as free-base equivalent, far below some high-micromolar mechanistic experiments. That does not prove inefficacy in MVA. It means approval is not enough, and the first experiment must start in a clinically anchored low-micromolar range.

## Slide 6 | 1:57-2:34
NB17 fixes our evidence ceiling before seeing N1002K: with six benign and five pathogenic controls, even perfect separation reaches only ClinGen Moderate strength, never Strong, and the assay threshold is locked from controls before unblinding. NB10 separately shows that false rescue must fail. Its synthetic moderate scenario assumes a 40 percent true reduction, while the decision threshold is 25 percent. Cytostasis and selective killing score zero clean rescues. Four replicates are a QC floor; our target is eight, with effect sizes and 95 percent confidence intervals.

## Slide 7 | 2:34-3:00
For the family and clinical team, the answer is simple. The BUB1B pair is the leading hypothesis, not a proven diagnosis or treatment. The cheapest decisive next test is parental genotyping: in trans, the recessive model becomes much stronger; in cis, the search reopens. Track 2 provides a pre-specified way to continue only when the biology supports it, and to stop when it does not.

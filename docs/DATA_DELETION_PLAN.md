# Controlled-data deletion plan

Hackathon close: 2026-10-24 23:59 UTC.
Deletion deadline: 2026-11-23 23:59 UTC.

Within 30 days of hackathon close, delete from participant-controlled systems:
- VCF/BAM/CRAM files and indexes;
- copies, slices, subsets and reformats carrying the child's genome;
- genotype-scale filtered/annotated variant tables;
- caches, notebook state and intermediate files carrying genome-wide genotypes;
- stored prompts/logs under participant control containing pasted blocks of variant data;
- any model artifacts trained directly on raw genomic data.

Retain only permitted findings and non-reconstructive outputs: ranked candidate variants, HPO terms, gene/pathway/mechanism analyses, drug hypotheses, sanitized code, report, pitch and leaderboard/submission records.

After deletion, perform a second search of participant-controlled storage and email the organiser at RarediseaserealkidMVAhackathon2026@synapse.org to confirm deletion.
